About JobBubble

JobBubble is an Android job-discovery application designed to help people find nearby employment opportunities using an interactive map.

Users can explore jobs by location, distance, pay, job type, and source.

How It Works

JobBubble displays available job opportunities geographically so users can quickly understand where jobs are located and discover opportunities near them.

Job Data

JobBubble may display job listings supplied by approved job-data providers and other authorized sources. Users may be directed to the original provider or employer website to complete an application.

Privacy

JobBubble may use device location when permission is granted in order to show jobs near the user. Location information is used for job-search functionality.

JobBubble does not sell personal information.
