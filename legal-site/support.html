JobBubble Support

If you need help with JobBubble, have a problem with your account, or want to report an issue, contact us by email.

Email JobBubble Support

Support email: jobbubbleapp@gmail.com

When contacting support

For faster troubleshooting, include a short description of the problem, what you expected to happen, what actually happened, and your Android device model if the issue appears device-specific. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, or other sensitive credentials.

Privacy and account deletion

For information about how JobBubble handles data, read the Privacy Policy. To request deletion of your JobBubble account and associated data, use the Account & Data Deletion page.
