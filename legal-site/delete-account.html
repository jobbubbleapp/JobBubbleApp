Account & Data Deletion

Delete your JobBubble account and data

You can request deletion of your JobBubble account and the personal data associated with that account without reinstalling the app.

How to request deletion

Use the button below to email JobBubble Support from the email address associated with your JobBubble account. The email subject is prefilled to identify it as an account deletion request.

Request Account Deletion

If the button does not open your email app, send an email manually to jobbubbleapp@gmail.com with the subject JobBubble Account Deletion Request.

What happens next

  • We may ask you to verify that you control the account before completing deletion.
  • After verification, the JobBubble account and account-associated personal data will be deleted or de-identified.
  • Limited information may be retained only when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal, or regulatory obligations.

Do not send your password, one-time authentication codes, or other sensitive credentials.

Need help?

For other questions, visit JobBubble Support or review the Privacy Policy.
