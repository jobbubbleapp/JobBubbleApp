Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JobBubble handles information when you use the JobBubble Android app and related services.

Information JobBubble may collect

  • Account information: information used to create or sign in to your account, such as your email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Profile and job preferences: information you choose to save, such as your saved location, search radius, job categories, pay preferences, education, licenses or certifications, and saved job preferences.
  • Location information: device location or a location you enter, when you choose to provide it. JobBubble uses location to search for nearby jobs, calculate distance, and place jobs on the map.
  • Saved and account-related data: information associated with features such as saved jobs and profile settings.
  • Technical data: limited device, network, crash, security, or diagnostic information that may be processed by service providers needed to operate the app.

How information is used

JobBubble uses information to provide and improve the app, authenticate users, save settings and profile information, search for jobs, estimate job locations, calculate distance, show map results, provide support, protect the service, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

Service providers and data sharing

JobBubble may send or make information available to service providers only as needed to operate app features. These may include authentication and database providers, mapping and geocoding services, hosting providers, and job-listing data providers. For example, location or search terms may be sent to services used to return relevant jobs or map results.

JobBubble does not sell your personal information.

Google and Firebase services

JobBubble may use Google services, including Firebase authentication or database services and Google mapping services. Information processed by those services is also subject to the applicable Google privacy terms.

Data retention

Account-associated information is retained while needed to provide JobBubble and for legitimate operational, security, or legal purposes. When a verified account deletion request is completed, JobBubble will delete or de-identify account-associated personal data unless limited retention is required for security, fraud prevention, legal, or regulatory reasons.

Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your JobBubble account and associated data using our Account & Data Deletion page. You do not need to reinstall the app to submit a web-based deletion request.

Security

JobBubble uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

JobBubble is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as JobBubble changes. The updated date at the top of this page will show when the policy was last revised.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to jobbubbleapp@gmail.com.
